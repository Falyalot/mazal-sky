Juliana, founder of Mazal Way

It started with David

When I was looking for child care for David, I wondered who would notice he was upset before he had the words to say why.

I wanted someone who knew what made him laugh and why that little toy car had to come along today.

Read Juliana’s story

I’m Juliana. I was 43 the first time I held my son, David. It took me years to become a mom, and it wasn’t easy. These days I hear “Mom” all the time at home. I still remember how long I waited to hear it.

When I was looking for child care for David, I kept coming back to one question: if he got upset and didn’t have the words to tell anyone why, who would notice? I have a bachelor’s degree in nursing. Working with patients taught me to pick up on the little signs that someone needs help, even before they ask. That’s the kind of care I wanted for David.

That’s how Mazal Way, my home daycare in Winnetka, began. I want every child here to have a grown-up who knows what makes them laugh, how to comfort them, and why that little toy car had to come along today. When you ask how the day went, I want to tell you a story that makes you smile and say, “That sounds just like my kid.”
